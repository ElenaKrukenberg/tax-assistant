# Every Expense carries the official form line it belongs to

Each Expense records the Anlage N line it is entered on, alongside its category
and amount. The lines were pinned against the form itself, not inferred from its
Anleitung: the commute in Zeilen 27–34, repeating in eight-line blocks for a
second and third workplace; Arbeitsmittel and AfA in 54–56; the
Homeoffice-Tagespauschale in 58 or 59 depending on whether another workplace was
available; Fortbildungskosten in 60; and everything else in the free "Sonstiges"
rows 62–63, totalled in 64 — Zeile 61, which looks generic, is ferry and flight
costs only. The immediate reason is the report: it can tell the user which line to copy each
figure into, which is what the product claims to do. The real reason is that the
long-term goal is transmitting a return electronically, the way WISO and Taxfix
do, and a figure without a target line is not transmittable data. Adding the
mapping now is one field and a table of a few entries; adding it later means
retrofitting it onto every case already stored, and re-deciding for each figure
where it was supposed to go.
